## Key Literature Gaps Addressed by Project Kinetica

### 1. Spatial Perspective & Continuous Queue Perception Gap
Existing computer vision and sensor-based systems (`sciencedirect_s240589632032629x`, `ieee_11380918`, `li_yolo_its_2023`) either rely on discrete point sensors (inductive loops/radars) or measure vehicles strictly in 2D image pixel space ($[x_1, y_1, x_2, y_2]$). This produces severe perspective distortion and cannot accurately capture spatial queue density along the roadway.
**Kinetica Solution:** Kinetica incorporates camera calibration and planar homography transformation ($H$) in `vision/project.py`, converting bounding box bottom-centers to real-world ground coordinates in meters ($m$) and spatial density ($\text{veh/m}$).

### 2. Unverified Stochastic Arrival Assumptions Gap
State-of-the-art adaptive controllers (`iet_itr2_12518`, `liang_stochastic_atsc_2020`) formulate green extensions on the theoretical assumption of stationary Poisson arrivals without testing whether real-world traffic conforms to this distribution in real-time.
**Kinetica Solution:** Kinetica computes a rolling Exponentially Weighted Moving Average (EWMA) arrival rate ($\lambda$) and continuously runs an automated Chi-Square goodness-of-fit test (`actuation/arrival_model.py`) to mathematically verify the validity of the Poisson assumption.

### 3. Single-Intersection Isolated Override vs. Multi-Node Corridor Preemption Gap
Conventional emergency preemption systems (`sciencedirect_s240589632032629x`, `ieee_11490570`, `ijsrem_ev_prioritization`) operate on isolated, single-intersection logic, causing emergency vehicles to encounter repeated red lights and stop-and-go delays at downstream junctions.
**Kinetica Solution:** Kinetica implements directed-graph corridor path projection via NetworkX (`preemption/graph_router.py`), predicting downstream headings and pre-clearing coordinated green waves across multi-intersection corridors ahead of arrival.

### 4. Rigid FIFO Overrides & Non-Priority Starvation Gap
Prior priority implementations (`ieee_11490570`, `goodall_priority_v2i_2022`) utilize rigid FIFO overrides or static priority weights. During emergency platoons or heavy traffic bursts, cross-street lanes experience infinite delay and severe starvation.
**Kinetica Solution:** Kinetica establishes a dynamic Max-Heap priority queue (`preemption/heap.py`) featuring an anti-starvation aging multiplier ($+0.05 \cdot w$) alongside multi-tier class multipliers (Ambulance/Police: $1000\times$, School Van: $50\times$, Standard: $1\times$), guaranteeing bounded maximum wait times for all lanes.

### 5. Anecdotal Simulation Averages vs. Rigorous Statistical Hypothesis Testing Gap
The vast majority of adaptive traffic control literature reports unvalidated average delay reductions without distribution testing, variance analysis, or statistical significance verification (`chambers_its_benchmarking_2021`).
**Kinetica Solution:** Kinetica embeds a rigorous statistical validation engine (`analytics/hypothesis_test.py`) that performs automated Shapiro-Wilk normality testing, dynamically selects between Welch's paired $t$-test and the non-parametric Mann-Whitney U test, and asserts statistical significance ($p < 0.05$) against baseline fixed-timer controllers.
